How to play

  1. Download RPG-Launcher-Setup.zip, unzip it and run Install RPG Launcher.bat.
  2. Open the RPG Launcher icon on your desktop.
  3. Register (first time) or log in with your username and password, then press Start Game.
  4. TLauncher opens on the Forge 1.20.1 version. Your TLauncher nickname must be the same as your username. Press Play.
  5. In the main menu press Start Game: you join the server.

News

Welcome! Level up, choose a job, clear gates and dungeons. World bosses appear in server events: watch the announcements.

Rules

  • Be respectful to every player.

  • No griefing or stealing.
  • Never share your password.
